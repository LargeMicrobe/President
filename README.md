# President

An exercise in game dev





The following list encompasses the rules for the card game called President:



* The game is played with a standard deck of playing cards, without any jokers
* It can be played with 3-5 players but works best with 4 or 5.
* Although it can be played for a single game, typically a session of president lasts multiple games
* The deck is shuffled and is then dealt, in its entirety, to the players at the table
* The object of the game is to get rid of all of the cards in your hand, this is called "going out"
* The first round begins with whoever has the three of clubs
* Whenever a round starts whoever begins may play whatever card they wish
* Cards must be played in ascending order (a 4 can be played after a 3 or a 4, but not after a 5 or greater)
* 2 is the highest rank, 3 is the lowest
* A player may only play cards of a single rank in one turn (You may play any number of 4s, but you cannot play a 4 and a 5)
* You must play at least as many cards as the person before you. (You can play one or two 4s on top of a 3 but you cannot play a 4 on top of two 3s)
* If you cannot, or do not want to play a card, you can pass
* If all other players pass, the deck clears and whoever played the last card starts a new round
* Example sequence below:





P1: 3 of clubs

P2: 4 of hearts

P3: 5 of clubs and 5 of spades

P1: 6 of hearts and 6 of diamonds

P2: 10 of spades, hearts, and clubs

P3: (does not have three cards of anything better than a 10 nor any trump cards) - Pass

P1: Ace of spades, hearts, and diamonds

P2: Pass

P3: Pass

P1: Starts new round with 4 of diamonds



Additional rules:

* If two cards of the same rank are played in sequence by players, the next following player's turn is skipped:



P1: 3 of clubs

P2: 3 of hearts

P3: SKIPPED

P1: 3 of diamonds



* If any sequence of events causes all four cards of the same rank to be played on top of each other, the deck is cleared, and whoever
* &nbsp;	Typically this occurs either after multiple players play them in sequence, or if one person has 4 of the same rank card
* Keep playing after the first person goes out, the order each player finishes matters for the next game
* There are four special titles for the winners and losers of the first game depending on the order they go out in:
* &nbsp;	First: President
* &nbsp;	Second: Vice President
* &nbsp;	Second to last: Vice Dickass
* &nbsp;	Last: Dickass
* Between rounds the President and Dickass exchange cards, as do the Vice President and Vice Dickass
* The president gives their 2 worst cards, whereas the Dickass gives their 2 best cards
* The Vice President gives their 1 worst card, whereas the Vice Dickass gives their 1 best card
* For a 3 player game there is no Vice President or Vice Dickass, but you do use their rules for card exchanges (1 each)
